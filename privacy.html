Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 4, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 4, 2026

GravinGo ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application GravinGo (the "App"), available on Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: If you sign in using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we receive your display name, email address, and profile picture from the authentication provider. We do not store your password.
  • In-App Purchases: Transaction records for in-app purchases (cosmetic items, key packs, ad removal). Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we do not collect or store your credit card or payment details.
  • Support Communications: If you contact us via email, we may retain the content of your messages and your email address to respond to your inquiry.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Game Data: High scores, achievement progress, unlocked items, inventory (coins, keys), gameplay statistics, control mode preferences, and session data. This data is stored to enable cloud save, leaderboards, and game progression features.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, language and locale settings, screen resolution, and timezone.
  • Usage Data: App launch times, session duration, feature usage frequency, screens viewed, and in-game events (e.g., game over, achievements unlocked).
  • Crash and Performance Data: Crash logs, error reports, and performance metrics to help us identify and fix bugs.
  • Advertising Data: Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android) if you have not opted out of ad tracking. We do not serve personalized ads by default.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Providers: When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive limited profile information as described above.
  • Analytics Providers: Aggregated and anonymized analytics data from Firebase Analytics.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each of which has its own privacy policy governing the use of your information:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication, Cloud Firestore (database), Analytics, Crashlytics (crash reporting). Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Serving banner, interstitial, and rewarded video advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC): Authentication service. Google Privacy Policy
  • Sign in with Apple (Apple Inc.): Authentication service. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store: App distribution and in-app purchase processing.

4. Advertising

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. By default, we serve non-personalized ads that do not use your personal data for ad targeting.

You may see the following types of ads:

  • Banner Ads: Displayed on non-gameplay screens.
  • Interstitial Ads: Full-screen ads displayed between game sessions.
  • Rewarded Video Ads: Optional ads you can watch to receive in-game rewards (e.g., continue a run).

You can remove all advertisements by purchasing the "Remove Ads" in-app product.

Ad Tracking: On iOS, we respect your App Tracking Transparency (ATT) preferences. If you decline tracking, no personalized ads will be served. On Android, you can opt out via your device's ad settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Firebase servers operated by Google, located in secure data centers. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Encryption of data at rest
  • Firebase Security Rules to restrict database access
  • Secure authentication via OAuth 2.0

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. Specifically:

  • Account and game data: Retained until you request deletion or delete your account.
  • Analytics data: Retained in aggregated/anonymized form for up to 14 months.
  • Crash logs: Retained for up to 90 days.
  • Support communications: Retained for up to 2 years after your last communication.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data and account.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your data.
  • Objection: Object to processing of your data for certain purposes.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at dev.talhacosar@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

GravinGo is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at dev.talhacosar@gmail.com. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

9. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States (where Google/Firebase servers are located). These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your jurisdiction.

By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to these countries. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • The right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information.
  • The right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell your personal information.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

11. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The legal bases for processing your data are described in Section 2 above. You may exercise your rights as described in Section 7.

If you believe we have not adequately addressed your data protection concerns, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

12. Do Not Track

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Since there is no accepted standard for how to respond to DNT signals, we do not currently respond to them. However, we serve non-personalized ads by default and respect iOS App Tracking Transparency preferences.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Your continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
